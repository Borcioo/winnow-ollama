    How to read this record

    Repeated calls use deterministic argmax selection; agreement and probability spread describe this run, not Bernoulli sampling uncertainty. A modal change is descriptive. “Accuracy” appears only where the scenario supplies an expected semantic answer. Moral choices have no supplied ground truth.
